# Crown & Bounty: the world

Broad strokes (29 Sep 2026). The details get settled as each act is built, and they'll change.

**Tone:** storybook fantasy in the spirit of HoMM2 and King's Bounty. It's funny and not too serious, with real
villains and stakes that start small and grow, all the way to dragons. Nobody turns anyone into a frog.

## The story

A hundred years ago, the old King (Osric's father) was a dragon's champion. When his dragon friend seized the
dragons' throne and turned bad, he slipped away with the rightful heir's egg. He hid it in the orb of his
sceptre, buried it, and tore its map into five pieces for five friends to keep. The dragon queen never knew.

- **Act I, the King's country.** The egg is due to hatch. The five friends' grandchildren have turned crook to dig
  up "the old King's treasure". King Osric knows what's really buried, and sends his new officer to bring them
  in, one map piece at a time. Aldric digs up the Sceptre of Order, and its orb is warm.
- **Act II, the islands.** The egg hatches, and the baby dragon decides Aldric is her dad. Baron Grimsby escapes
  from the Tower, steals her and sells her on, and she's sold up a chain of pirates, each richer and scarier than
  the last. Aldric follows with a crew of every race, and the King comes aboard with his throne and his goose,
  who can't stand the dragon. By the time Aldric rescues her, she's a teenager who has dreamed of who she is.
- **Act III, the dragons' mountains.** Four dragon lords hold her family's hoard, each on a mountain on the way to
  the usurper's throne. She wants revenge and her crown. Aldric wants the gold, the fame, and to keep an eye on
  her.
- **The ending.** She does what Aldric taught her by the way he treated the villains he beat. Either way she's
  queen: a kind one who comes home for the holidays, or the new terror of the mountains, which leaves Aldric
  knowing exactly how the old King felt.

## The three acts

| | Act I: the King's country | Act II: the islands | Act III: the dragons' mountains |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Villains** | five small-time crooks, Grimsby first | Grimsby, then the pirates who buy her | four dragon lords, then the usurper |
| **Troops** | humans, elves and dwarves, and a wild monster or two for spice | adds orcs and trolls, warlocks and the dead, mages, merfolk, lizardfolk, desert folk and sea beasts | adds dragons and drakes, Wesnoth's giants, and legendary heroes |
| **Battles** | masses: lines and blocks | crews: tricks and counters | legends: a few huge stacks |
| **Court** | the throne room | the deck of Aldric's ship | a tent on a dragon's mountain |

## Act I first

What changes in today's game:

- **Aldmoor stays, and grows** (see [act1/aldmoor.md](act1/aldmoor.md)), with Grimsby, the goose and its people.
  Grimsby is now the grandson of one of the old King's friends, and the only crook who knows what the treasure
  really is.
- **Mother Mirrow and Aunt Bramble go,** along with Grimsby's second commission and the elopement. Commissions II to
  V get four new crooks (see [act1/crooks.md](act1/crooks.md)), each with a map piece and a different people behind
  him: Black Hollis's outlaws in the Fenmarch, which keeps its map, Elowen's elves, Gorm Goldtooth's dwarves, and
  Rufus the First's knights.
- **New troops:** elves and dwarves (Wesnoth's), and a wild monster or two in each province.
- **The dig** at the end of commission V finds the Sceptre, with an egg for its orb. Until Act II exists, the
  campaign ends there: to be continued. A line or two earlier on should hint that the treasure is more than gold.

## One commission, start to end

1. **At court,** the King gives the commission, and his clerk hands over a WANTED poster.
2. **In the province,** the crook's people stand in the way. You find mysteries you can't solve yet, and their
   answers lie somewhere far off.
3. **The crook's people move:** he rides out when you hurt him, and his hunters come looking for you.
4. **At the lair,** talking him round has a price (the Courtier's parley). He fights, but he can't be touched:
   beat his army, and he's yours.
5. **Back at court,** the King remembers what you did, and a boon can carry someone forward (Old Nan's charm, for
   instance). Then comes the next piece of the map.

This comes from playtest 01 (the Courtier in Aldmoor).

## Artur's rules

- **Hint, don't tell.** Clues are quiet. *"Whatever you find, don't let it get cold"* was far too loud.
- **Heroes and captains fight, but can't be touched** (#36). Aldric, the villains and the enemy's captains stand
  behind their troops, not in the line, and act from there. The Ranger shoots, and the Wizard throws bolts and
  casts; the Knight rides out, strikes and rides back. Villains and captains fight the same way, each in his own
  style. No blow and no spell can reach them, friend or foe (a Fireball passes over them), so their blows get no
  strike back. Each needs his army: when it's beaten, so is he. Aldric retreats, and a villain is taken.
- **The Courtier is a bard** (#42). He has no attack: he bribes, taunts and sings. Whether he's renamed the Bard,
  with a new face and lines, is still open.
- **Captains are the enemy's** (#15). Aldric is the only hero you ever have. Every villain has captains (Rook the
  Huntsman leads Grimsby's wolves) who fight the same way.
- **No gimmicks** that cost code and add little. Scarecrows that spy for Grimsby were one.
