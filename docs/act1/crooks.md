# The four crooks

Commissions II to V (#13). Artur agreed these broad strokes on 29 Sep 2026; the details get settled as each one is
built, and none of it is built yet.

## All four

- **Five friends.** The old King gave the pieces of his map to his thief, his archer, his smith, his champion and
  Grimsby's grandfather. Their grandchildren have turned crook to dig up his treasure. Each thinks it's something
  else, and each is a little bit right. Only Grimsby knows.
- **Aldmoor's shape:** a province the size of the new Aldmoor, a barrier to cross, three weeks, and a lair you can
  take around day 21 (in theory, on day 1).
- **A mirror.** Each crook fights from behind his troops the way one of Aldric's backgrounds does: Hollis like the
  Courtier, Elowen the Ranger, Gorm the Wizard and Rufus the Knight. The abilities are data already.
- **The race.** Every lair holds a letter from Grimsby in the Tower, offering to buy its piece "for sentimental
  reasons", for more each time.
- **Old choices open new ways, never the only way,** since a commission can start with the bot's hero.

The armies go from rabble (many, cheap and tricky) to skill (shooters, casters, walking trees), to armour (shields,
thunderers, berserkers, gryphons), to the best of everything. The wild monsters are dragonflies and boars in the fen,
giant spiders in the Wychwood, gryphons on Silverfell and great wolves on the downs. The bog goblins and the troll
leave the Fenmarch for Act II, as WORLD.md's table has it.

## II. Black Hollis, the Bandit King (#87)

> **WANTED: Black Hollis, the Bandit King,** for robbing the King's roads, burning Tuttle Mill, and holding the tax
> collector to ransom. Twice. Reward: **2,000 gold**, alive. *Bring an army. He has one.*

- **His grandad,** Old Tom Hollis, was the old King's thief, and died a monk at St Wendel's. He left his piece to
  Brother Anselm, and Hollis stole it. He thinks it's gold: *"Grandad said it was the best thing he ever pinched."*
- **The Fenmarch keeps its map.** Mirrow's hut becomes the Holt, where Hollis sits on a stolen pew in a wicker crown.
  The goblins become his reed-runners, his toll-keepers take the troll's bridge (*"Toll is one horse. Or two
  knights."*), and the punt still slips past.
- **People:** Thugs, Footpads, Thieves, poachers and highwaymen; Hollis is Wesnoth's Highwayman. **Captains:** Nell
  the Eel (a Rogue) leads the reed-runners, and Grout (an Outlaw) keeps the bridge.
- **He fights** like a Courtier gone bad: he pays your men to go home with the King's own taxes, and jeers. **He
  moves** out when you hurt him, Nell hunts the causeway, and every payday another road gets a toll.
- **Mysteries.** Eelby paid the ransom twice, and his men never saw a penny: it's in a hollow willow far across the
  fen. Take it, or show his men and half of them walk out. Old Tom's cell is locked, and its key is in an eel trap
  far down the fen; his picks inside open the Holt's water gate.
- **Parley:** Anselm's letter gets him talking, and his price is his cut, in gold. **Court:** *"My tax collector is
  home, and charging me for his time."* He rides with you after, and wages cost a tenth less.
- **The egg:** Old Tom's sack, in his cell, still smells of smoke.

## III. Elowen of the Hollow Oak (#88)

> **WANTED: Elowen of the Hollow Oak, aged ninety-three** (young, for an elf), for growing an oak through the King's
> hunting lodge (overnight), shutting the woodcutters of Axebury in with brambles, and taking every swan on the
> King's river. Reward: **3,000 gold**, alive. *Mind the trees. Some of them walk.*

- **Her grandmother,** Hazel, was the old King's archer, and still lives at the far end of the wood, three hundred and
  six and cross. Elowen thinks it's a seed that will grow her wood over the kingdom: *"Grandmother says the trees can
  hear it."*
- **People:** Elvish Fighters, Archers, Shamans and Scouts, and Woses; Elowen is an Elvish Sharpshooter.
  **Captains:** Old Oakheart (an Ancient Wose) leads the woses, and her cousin Ferrin (an Elvish Outrider) the scouts.
- **She fights** like a Ranger: she shoots from behind her line, and her archers loose a volley first. **She moves**
  through the woods, where you can't follow unless you can walk them too. Ferrin's scouts always know where you are,
  and a grove of woses walks a little nearer the King's castle every day.
- **Mysteries.** Brambles shut Axebury in. Burn them (Fireball) and the elves never forgive you, or bring Hazel's word
  from the far end of the wood, and the woodcutters show you the old rides to the Oak. The walking woses remember the
  old King: something of his, found far off, sends half of them to sleep.
- **Parley:** say her full name the way Hazel does (*"Elowen Periwinkle Thistledown!"*), and she comes down. Her
  price is a promise that the King's road goes round the wood. **Court:** *"Round the wood? The whole wood?"* The King
  keeps it, and takes the new road out of your reward. Her cousins, elvish archers, can be hired next time.
- **The egg:** the trees can hear it.

## IV. Gorm Goldtooth (#89)

> **WANTED: Gorm Goldtooth, Master of the Mountain** (self-appointed), for shutting the King's silver mines,
> tunnelling into the cellars of Castle Fellgate, and sending the King a bill for the mountain. Reward: **4,000
> gold**, alive. *Don't follow him underground.*

- **His grandad,** Old Brokk, was the old King's smith, and made the Sceptre. Gorm thinks it's the finest stone Brokk
  ever set, and he's digging up the mountains for it, in the wrong place, as Grimsby dug the heath.
- **People:** Dwarvish Fighters, Thunderers, Guardsmen, Ulfserkers and Gryphon Riders; Gorm is a Dwarvish
  Runemaster. **Captains:** Hilde (a Dragonguard) holds the pass, where the Dragonguard have kept watch for a hundred
  years, against what nobody remembers; Skarn (a Gryphon Master) rides out on his gryphon, strikes and flies back.
- **He fights** like a Wizard: two runes a round, Stone Skin for his men and thunder for yours. **He moves** under the
  mountain, into one mine and out of another, and Skarn's riders fly over crags and rivers to find you.
- **Mysteries.** Brokk's forge has been cold for a hundred years, and his old dwarves won't light it *"till the stone
  comes home"*. His ledger, in his ruined smithy across the fells, says it went west with the old King: show them,
  and they forge you something. Wild gryphons nest where nobody can climb, but they come to the whistle of the
  old gryphon master Gorm threw out, far off, and join you.
- **Parley:** the ledger (or his cousin's helm, from Aldmoor's mine) gets him talking, and his price is the finest
  thing you carry: he chooses. **Court:** *"The dwarves have sent me a bill."* His miners go ahead, and dig you a
  tunnel in the last province.
- **The egg:** the ledger says *"Setting for an orb. Gold. Leave room for it to grow."*

## V. Rufus the First (#90)

> **WANTED: Sir Rufus Brannoc, who calls himself King Rufus the First,** for taking the King's castles, minting
> pennies with his own face on them, and knighting his horse. Reward: **5,000 gold**, alive. *He has knights. And
> the horse.*

- **His grandad,** Sir Brannoc, was the old King's champion, and lost his lance in the fen (Hollis's reed-runners
  have it). Rufus thinks it's the crown: *"Whoever digs up the Sceptre is King. Grandad said so."*
- **People:** Knights, Lancers and Cavalrymen, and whoever the others left, paid in Gorm's silver; Rufus is a Grand
  Knight. **Captains:** Sir Giles (a Lancer), and any captain who got away from an earlier crook.
- **He fights** like a Knight: he rides out, charges and rides back, and his knights charge too. **He moves** to war:
  every payday he takes another of the King's castles or villages, which then raises troops for him, and he rides
  out when you take one back.
- **Mysteries.** His claim is the old King's will, nailed to Oldcrown's gate. The forger who wrote it lives far off,
  still wants paying, and will say so; then his knights go home. Warmwell's well has run warm since the old King's
  day, and nobody knows why. The last piece of the map does.
- **Parley:** once his knights have gone (or you give back his grandad's lance), he'll talk, and his price is to
  kneel to him, once, as one king to another. **The dig** is under Warmwell's well: the Sceptre, and its orb is
  warm. **Court:** *"You knelt to him?"* Then the King holds the Sceptre and goes quiet, and the goose hisses at it.
  To be continued.
- **The egg:** a figure cut in the chalk, which the locals call the White Horse. It doesn't look much like a horse.

## How to build them

**Hand-made, like Aldmoor, one at a time,** each in a session of its own once Aldmoor is the golden example, the
Fenmarch first. Chests, gold and lone trees can still fall where the seed puts them.

| | Hand-made (chosen) | Set pieces on generated land | Generated, as today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Feels** | new every time | new places, on land that looks alike | the same castle, tower, mine and mill, shuffled |
| **Answers far off** | far because they're put there | needs rules for "far" and "over the river" | a note at the tower |
| **Cost** | each about the bigger Aldmoor (#77, #75, #76, #78), plus a people and a land | a generator that knows regions and barriers, then each set piece: more than four hand-made maps | a template each: the cheapest |

Each needs his people from Wesnoth (the crook, two captains, four or five troops), his land drawn in code, one new
way to move on the map, a tune, a theme and portraits. Hollis is the cheapest, since the Fenmarch and its outlaws are
half there; Gorm is the dearest, with a new people, a new land, tunnels and fliers. Until each is built, today's
generated commission can wear his poster, so the story reads right from end to end.

## Decided (Artur, 29 Sep)

1. **Hand-made like Aldmoor,** one at a time, the Fenmarch first: not set pieces on generated land, nor generated as
   today.
2. **Rufus the First is the fifth crook:** not a necromancer brought forward from Act II, nor a crook of one of the
   three peoples.
3. **Each crook fights like one of Aldric's backgrounds,** not in a style of his own.
